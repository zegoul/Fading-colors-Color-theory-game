AQUARIUM FIELD GUIDEDIVE BRIEFING · 01

Before you dive

Keep the aquarium alive by reading carefully, planning ahead, and protecting every room's color.

  1. 01Protect the colors.Don't let the color of the rooms fade.
  2. 02Read the clues.Field notes are spread across the map. Read them in place to unlock quests; press E again to close a note.
  3. 03Complete each main quest.Main quests are located on the pearl tables in Rooms A, B, and C.
  4. 04Choose carefully.Failing a main quest locks that room and makes every other active room fade faster.
  5. 05Try side quests.Optional S.T tasks can reward you with a 12-second pause for one room. Finish side quests before the final main task.
